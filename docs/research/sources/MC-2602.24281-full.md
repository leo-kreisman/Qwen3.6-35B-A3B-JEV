## 6 Conclusion
In this paper, we present Memory Caching (MC), a simple technique applicable to all recurrent neural networks, that caches a subset of memory state, allowing subsequent tokens directly attend to its past relevant tokens. Our experiments show improvements over a subset of baselines.
A lot of choices in this paper have been made to keep the resulting model as simple as possible, better showing the effect of memory caching idea. However, in future work, more expressive pooling or routing mechanism can be used to further enhance the performance.
